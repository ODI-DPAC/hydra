## Introduction to Hydra


All new (non-SAO) Hydra users are required to watch the video available at [this link](https://sinet.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/SI-HydraHPC/IQAUdkLjjxHIQrfpd5Txeaq8AXGTM1qw2I5J7aqE3OpZcDk?e=8dh9TO&nav=eyJyZWZlcnJhbEluZm8iOnsicmVmZXJyYWxBcHAiOiJTdHJlYW1XZWJBcHAiLCJyZWZlcnJhbFZpZXciOiJTaGFyZURpYWxvZy1MaW5rIiwicmVmZXJyYWxBcHBQbGF0Zm9ybSI6IldlYiIsInJlZmVycmFsTW9kZSI6InZpZXcifX0%3D). The purpose of this training is to:


1. Provide an overview of Hydra, the Smithsonian High Performance Computing Cluster
2. Give Hydra users the basic information to get started using Hydra
3. Inform users about the Hydra usage policies (posted [here](hydra-policies.md)) and ensure that they agree to abide by those policies


For questions concerns about the training, please contact us at [si-hpc\@si.edu](mailto:si-hpc@si.edu) (non-SAO users) or [hpc\@cfa.harvard.edu](mailto:hpc@cfa.harvard.edu) (SAO users).


Other training materials are available in this [GitHub repository](https://github.com/SmithsonianWorkshops/Hydra-introduction).


We also offer quarterly synchronous workshops - these are announced via the HPCC-L email list.


## Advanced Workshops


We also offer advanced Hydra workshops on a semi regular basis.


Past workshops are accessible at this [GitHub repository](https://github.com/SmithsonianWorkshops/advanced-hydra-workshops), where you can find slides, hand-on material and recording:


- [Job Arrays](https://github.com/SmithsonianWorkshops/advanced-hydra-workshops/tree/main/job_arrays) (given on 2023-06-21)
- [Shell scripting and useful tools](https://github.com/SmithsonianWorkshops/advanced-hydra-workshops/tree/main/scripting) (e.g. sed, awk, grep, ...., given on 2023-05-10)
- [Installing software and writing modules on Hydra](https://github.com/SmithsonianWorkshops/advanced-hydra-workshops/tree/main/install_sw%2Bm) (given on 2023-03-22)
- [Conda](https://github.com/SmithsonianWorkshops/advanced-hydra-workshops/tree/main/conda) on Hydra (given on 2023-02-15


Future advanced workshops will be announced via the HPCC-L email list.
